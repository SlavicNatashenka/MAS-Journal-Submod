======================

Put PNG or JPG images in this folder:

  game/Submods/Journal Submod/Photos/

Then in-game:
1. Open the Journal button
2. Switch to Photo mode
3. Press Browse
4. Pick an image, crop/size/move it, then Place

- Real .png / .jpg files work best 
- After adding new files, you may need to reopen the game so they appear
- Photos you place are saved with your journal pages in persistent data
- Original files in this folder are never deleted by the submod
